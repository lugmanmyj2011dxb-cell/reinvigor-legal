What this app is ReinVigor is a self-management and focus tool for adults 18 and over. It helps you track habits, ride out urges, and see your own progress. It is not a medical product. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. If you are struggling, please consult a qualified professional. 2. The short version No account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or any identifier. Your data stays on your device, encrypted with AES-256. The encryption key lives in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). Our servers never see your data in plain text. If you turn on encrypted backup, only ciphertext goes to your own iCloud or Google Drive — we do not hold the key. No analytics. No advertising SDKs. We don't track what you do inside the app or sell anything to anyone. You can delete everything, anytime, with one tap. It's gone. 3. We do not hold the decryption key. If you lose your backup code, the data is unrecoverable. Anonymous buddy (Pro, V2 — not yet live) Will send only "action event" records (type + local hour) to a matched peer. No text, no totals, no name, no location. Events auto-delete after 30 days. Stealth mode (Pro) Changes the app icon and notification text to a neutral appearance. Local-only visual change. No data is sent. 6. Your rights Access your data (on your device — open the app) Delete your data (Me > Privacy center > Delete all my data — one tap) Export your data (encrypted backup file is yours) Withdraw consent for optional features (toggle off) Because your data is encrypted on your own device and we don't hold a key, we cannot access or retrieve it. This is by design. 7. Children ReinVigor is for adults 18+. We use App Store and Google Play age ratings and signals. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. 8. International transfers Your data stays on your device by default. Encrypted backup goes to your own cloud storage. We do not transfer data to our own servers. 9. Retention On-device data: Retained until you delete it. V2 buddy events: 30 days, then automatic deletion. Purchase records: Retained by Apple/Google per their policies. 10. Security Your data is encrypted with AES-256-CBC. The key is generated with a cryptographically secure random number generator and stored in iOS Keychain (Secure Enclave-backed) or Android Keystore (hardware-backed). App Lock adds biometric/passcode protection. Stealth mode disguises the app icon and notifications. 11.
